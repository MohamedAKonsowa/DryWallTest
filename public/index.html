Jobs We Take

Common Drywall Patch & Repair Jobs

If your wall has a hole, crack, stain, or damaged sheet — that's our day-to-day work. Small patch jobs and multi-room repairs are both welcome.

  • Nail & screw holes

    Picture hangers, TV mounts, and old anchor holes patched smooth and paint-ready.

  • Doorknob & large holes

    Doorknob punches, pet damage, and fist-sized holes repaired with proper backing and mud.

  • Crack repair

    Seam cracks, corner cracks, and hairline ceiling cracks taped and finished to blend in.

  • Water damage

    Stained or soft drywall cut out, replaced, and finished after the leak is fixed.

  • Sheet replacement

    Bad sections or full sheets replaced, taped, mudded, and blended to the surrounding wall.

  • Ceiling patches

    Ceiling holes from fixtures, fans, and old repairs patched flat and ready for paint.

Common Questions

Louisville Drywall & Painting LLC FAQ

What drywall patching and repair jobs do you take?

Nail holes, doorknob holes, large holes, crack repair, water-damaged drywall, sheet replacement, ceiling patches, and texture blending. We also hang new drywall, finish walls, and paint after a patch when you need it.

What areas do you serve?

We serve Louisville and surrounding areas, plus rural communities across Kentucky and Southern Indiana. If you're not sure whether we cover your town, contact us with your location and we'll let you know.

How far will your team travel for a job?

We travel throughout the Louisville metro area and to rural towns in Kentucky and Southern Indiana. Call (502) 218-0426 or send your town through the contact form — we'll confirm whether we can come to you.

How much do drywall patch jobs cost?

Every patch is different — a few nail holes cost less than water damage or full sheet replacement. We provide a free on-site estimate with a clear labor quote before any work starts. You buy your own materials.

Do I buy my own materials?

Yes. You purchase your own drywall, joint compound, tape, primer, and paint. We charge for labor only — that keeps your total cost down and lets you choose the products you want.

How do I get a free drywall estimate?

Schedule online using the form below, call us at (502) 218-0426, or email quotes@louisvilledrywallpaints.org. We provide free on-site estimates across Kentucky and Southern Indiana.
